# Controls v3: STANDARD mobile third-person controls (owner, Nov 3, 8:15 AM PT)
Owner on the cozy camera (v2): "I really hate the controller. I don't like it. I want to be able to use it how you normally would, right? I don't like the buttons that they're there. They're terrible."
Earlier (v1): "it's more swiveling around I was having trouble with." Plus "my controller keeps freezing... gets stuck and forces me to move in a direction I don't want."
=> Replace the cozy scheme with the scheme every mobile 3D game uses. NO rotate buttons, NO look stick.

## Walk mode
- MOVE: a virtual joystick for the LEFT thumb. It floats: it appears where the left thumb lands anywhere in the left half of the screen (below the top HUD band, not on HUD buttons), with a visible base and knob; when idle a faint base rests at the usual bottom-left spot as a hint. Camera-relative movement (push up = walk away from the camera), analog speed (walk/run by distance), instant response. Lifting the thumb ALWAYS stops him.
- LOOK: drag with the RIGHT thumb anywhere in the right half of the screen to turn the camera: horizontal drag = yaw (about 1:1 feel, ~0.0065 rad/px at "normal"), vertical drag = pitch (about half that, clamped to a comfortable range so you can never look under the ground or straight down), light smoothing so it glides, no inertia overshoot. Works AT THE SAME TIME as the joystick (true multi-touch: left thumb walks while right thumb looks).
- NO auto-swing fighting the player. Setting "Camera follows behind you" (default OFF): when on, after 1.5 s without look input while walking mostly forward, the camera eases slowly behind him; never while turning or strafing.
- ZOOM: pinch with two fingers on the look side (or two non-stick fingers anywhere); mouse wheel on desktop. The existing +/- zoom buttons may stay (small), but nothing else.
- TAP: a quick tap (< ~10 px, < ~300 ms) on the world still does tap-to-go / interact / talk exactly as now; long-press as now. A tap never starts the stick or a look drag.
- Remove: the ↺ ↻ rotate buttons and TURN label, the #look rate stick, flick-to-rotate, the Cozy/Free selector. Settings > Camera becomes: Look sensitivity (slow / normal / fast), Invert vertical look (off), Camera follows behind you (off). Persist in SET; migrate SET.cam (any value) to the new scheme.
- Desktop: WASD/arrows move (camera-relative), mouse drag (any button) looks, wheel zooms, Q/E turn while held.
- Inside rooms (B12 room camera) and other CAM layers: the joystick still walks; look drags rotate within what the layer allows (or are ignored if the layer is locked); when the layer pops, the walk camera resumes where the player left it. Keep the B11/B23 occluder and two-shot fixes from v2 (fade/nudge when the fountain etc. blocks; talk two-shot).
## Never stuck (the freeze bug)
Every way JOY/LOOK/KEYS_DOWN can stay non-zero must be closed: release on pointerup/pointercancel/lostpointercapture wherever captured; a document-level touchend/touchcancel guard that clears the stick when its touch is gone (and everything when touches.length===0); clear on blur, visibilitychange, pagehide, orientationchange/resize, and whenever the stick or HUD is hidden or a sheet/card/talk card/Your Look/decorate/CAM layer takes over; a tiny allocation-free watchdog in frame() that releases the stick if its pointer is no longer active for > ~200 ms; tap-to-go cancels cleanly when the stick is used.
## Watch mode (following Mocha): unchanged (drag orbits, pinch zooms).
## Hooks/tests: __mocha.ctl() returns {scheme:'standard', yaw, pitch, dist, stick:{on,id,x,y}, look:{on,id}, follow, sens, invert}; /tmp/harness/test-stick.js covers the freeze scenarios; /tmp/harness/test-controls.js is REWRITTEN for v3 (left-thumb stick + right-thumb look simultaneously, no buttons present, tap still taps, pinch zooms, settings persist).
