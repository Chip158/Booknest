# Mothman — Single 15s Test Clip (Realistic Documentary Style)

Revised per feedback: drop the stylized/silhouette look, go realistic documentary reenactment with real-looking people and an off-screen narrator. This is scoped as **one 15-second test clip** — the cheapest possible way to see if the look and the story land before building anything bigger.

## The style, in plain terms

Think History Channel / Discovery Channel "unsolved mystery" reenactment — not animation, not neon noir. Real 1966-era clothing and cars, naturalistic lighting, a slightly grainy documentary film look. Real human actors, not silhouettes.

**One deliberate choice carried over from the last version:** Mothman itself stays glimpsed, not fully shown — a large dark shape and a pair of reflective red eyes caught in headlights, mostly obscured by darkness and motion. This isn't a style downgrade, it's how every real cryptid documentary actually shoots the creature (Bigfoot docs, Mothman docs — you never get a clean, well-lit full-body shot). It also happens to be what these AI video models render most convincingly — a fully lit, fully visible "monster" is where they tend to go cartoonish or malformed. And it sidesteps generating a specific real person's likeness: the actors are a generic young 1960s couple, not the real named witnesses.

## The single test clip — one story beat, two cuts, 15 seconds

**0:00–0:07 — Exterior:** A 1966 sedan driving alone down a two-lane rural road at night, headlights the only light source, trees close on both sides.

**0:07–0:15 — Cut to interior:** Back-seat POV, a real young couple in the front seat (period clothing, natural documentary performance), headlights sweep the roadside and catch a pair of reflective red eyes and a large dark shape for a split second — the woman turns, startled — then it's gone.

## ChatGPT prompt (2 reference keyframes, photorealistic)

Generate 2 photorealistic reference keyframes for a documentary-style video reenactment (History Channel / Discovery Channel "unsolved mystery" aesthetic — NOT animated, NOT stylized, NOT cartoon). Real human actors in accurate 1966 period clothing and hairstyles, a real 1966 sedan, naturalistic lighting, subtle 16mm documentary film grain.

KEYFRAME 1: Wide exterior shot of a 1966 sedan driving alone down a dark rural two-lane road at night, headlights the only source of light, dense trees close on both sides, West Virginia countryside.

KEYFRAME 2: Interior back-seat point-of-view of the same car, a young man and woman in the front seats in 1960s clothing, photorealistic and naturalistic (generic-looking actors, not any real named individual), headlights sweeping across roadside brush. In the very edge of the headlight beam, a large dark shape with two reflective red eyes is faintly visible — mostly obscured by shadow and motion blur, deliberately ambiguous, not a clearly rendered creature.

Vertical 9:16 framing. Photorealistic, not illustrated. No text rendered into the image.

## Seedance 2.0 prompt (single 15s generation, two cuts)

A 1966 sedan drives alone down a dark rural two-lane road at night, headlights sweeping across the pavement, dense trees close on both sides. Cut to: back-seat point-of-view inside the same car, a young man and woman in 1960s clothing in the front seats, natural documentary-style performance. The headlights sweep across roadside brush and catch a large dark shape with two reflective red eyes at the very edge of the light for a split second — mostly obscured by shadow and motion blur — the woman turns toward it, startled, then it's gone as the car keeps driving.

Style: photorealistic documentary reenactment, naturalistic lighting, subtle 16mm film grain — not animated, not stylized, not cartoon. Camera: shot 1 locked-off exterior wide; cut to shot 2 locked-off interior POV (no camera movement within either shot). Audio: ambient engine and road noise only, no dialogue, no music. Avoid: no cartoon or illustrated look, no warped hands/faces, no fully-lit or clearly-detailed creature, no jitter, no text overlay, no lip-synced speech.

Duration: 15 seconds. Aspect ratio: 9:16.

*(No dialogue is baked in on purpose — an off-screen narrator can't lip-sync to a mouth that isn't on camera, so the narration below gets added as a separate audio track over this clip, the same way you already record VO and lay it over footage in CapCut.)*

## Narrator script (fits the 15-second clip)

"November 15th, 1966. A young couple is driving home past an abandoned munitions plant outside Point Pleasant, West Virginia — when their headlights catch something that shouldn't exist."

(\~35 words, \~14–15 seconds at a natural narration pace — times out to the clip length. Record this yourself as "The Archivist" or generate a placeholder narrator voice for the test; swap in your real VO once you know the visual works.)

## How to actually run the cheap test

1.  Generate the 2 keyframes in ChatGPT (free).
2.  Feed keyframe 1 (or both, if the tool supports multi-image reference) into Seedance as the image-to-video reference, paste the prompt above, cap it at 15 seconds, and use the platform's lowest-cost/fastest tier for this first pass.
3.  Lay the narrator line over it in CapCut, same pipeline as always.
4.  Look at exactly two things before spending anything further: does it read as "realistic documentary" and not cartoon/uncanny, and does the reflective-eyes moment actually land as a hook. If both check out, this shot list is worth extending into the fuller story; if not, you've only spent one generation finding that out.
